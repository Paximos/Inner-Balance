## Scope

index.html, the whole site as one page. Mode: Persuade. Greek default, English toggle.

Audience: people in Heraklion (and online) reaching out to a psychologist, often for the first time, mostly on phones. Action: request a first session (mailto with choices filled) or call. Proof: real portrait, credentials timeline, three real testimonials, fees, hours, address.

## Direction contract

OWN-WORLD: Flat full-strength cover fields (cypress green, Aegean blue, saffron, pomegranate on paper white), one Greek-designed face (Commissioner, by Kostas Bartsokas) from heavy display to text, 1897 Admiralty engravings of Candia as frontispiece plates, square corners, no lines or shadows, saffron spent only on the action.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user's choice). One long page replacing the current four (Αρχική, Σχετικά, Ραντεβού, Επικοινωνία). Greek default with a full English toggle.

## Users

- Adults and teenagers (and their parents) in Heraklion, Crete, looking for a psychologist, often for the first time, often anxious about reaching out. Mostly on a phone.
- Couples and families looking for joint therapy.
- English-speaking residents of Crete who need therapy in English (a real testimonial comes from one).
- People outside Heraklion who need online sessions.

Their job: decide quickly whether this person is qualified, safe and approachable, understand cost and logistics, and take the first step (call or request an appointment) without friction.

## Product Principles

1. Lower the threshold: the first step must feel small, private and clear.
2. Show the person: a real face, real credentials, plain words.
3. Logistics are care: cost, duration, hours and place answered before anyone has to ask.
4. Confidentiality is stated, never dramatised.
